Privacy

Apollo Delta has no servers and no accounts of its own, and it collects nothing about you.

Your Reddit sign-in happens on Reddit's own page. The app keeps the resulting Reddit session on your device and sends it only to Reddit, to load and do what you ask (feeds, votes, comments, messages). Your password never passes through Apollo Delta. Signing out deletes the session from your device.

Sign in with Google opens Reddit's sign-in in a separate window of your Chrome, Edge or Brave, with a profile kept only for this. It's never your own browser profile. Settings → Accounts → Forget Google Sign-In deletes it.

Your settings, favorites, saved categories and reading history stay on your device (in Apollo Delta's data folder), per account.

Other services, only when you use them: link previews load the linked page's title and picture; Translate sends the text to Google Translate; checking for updates asks GitHub for the latest release; the app fetches its Reddit route list from this project's GitHub repository.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
